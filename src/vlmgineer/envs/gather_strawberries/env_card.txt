This is a description of the Scoop Starberries Environment. In this environment, a Franka robot is positioned on a brown table along with an open three-walled container filled with starberries. The open container does not have panels on the wall facing the robot and on top, leaving a 0.3 meters wide gap between the left and right container walls, where the robot can easily access the exposed starberries sitting on the table.
Each small starberries has a radius of 0.02 meters. The center of the container base is at coordinate [0.7, 0.0, 0.0], and the container spans a length of 0.3 meters in both the x and y directions, and a height of 0.2 meters. The container's back wall (away from the robot), which is at an x coordinate of 0.85 meters, provides a physical barrier that stops the starberries from rolling away from the robot.
The base of the Franka robot is at the origin of the world frame [0.0, 0.0, 0.0], and its end-effector is initialized at [0.4, 0.0, 0.1].
In this environment, you need to design a tool and motion that can scoop up as many starberries and lift them above a height_threshold of 0.3 meters. The performance is measured by the total height of all starberries.
Although the starberries are small, they will take up significant space quickly. Also, collision with the container walls usually results in tools getting stuck, so leave enough safety gaps in between. The environment contains a lot of starberries, and the goal is to create a strategy that ideally lifts all of the starberries above the height_threshold of 0.3 meters.
